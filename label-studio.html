Printing with the Niimbot iPhone app?
  1. In the Niimbot app tap Image, pick your saved label from Photo album, then tap Add.
  2. Set Contrast to 0 so no white outline appears.
  3. Drag the blue dot in the label's corner until the label fills the whole label, then Print.

Heads up: the Niimbot iPhone app prints at lower quality than the desktop version and shrinks every upload, so it has to be resized. Printing with the Niimbot M2 from a desktop computer gives better image quality and is easier, whether you design in BCS Label Studio or anywhere else.
